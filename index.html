Our Philosophy

The Art of Honest Ingredients

For over fifteen years, Chef Étienne Laurent has built Maison Doré on a simple belief: extraordinary food begins with respect for the ingredient. We work with small farms and local fishermen to bring the season's finest to your table.

Each dish blends classical French technique with modern restraint, served in an intimate room designed for conversation, celebration and discovery.
